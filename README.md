# Ecotourism Market Growth & Traveler Behaviour Analysis 2025
### Business Analysis Case Study | Raj Hamal | May 2026

---

## The Central Question
The global ecotourism market is worth $286 billion 
and growing at 15.5% CAGR. Yet only 33% of travelers 
who want sustainable tourism actually book it. Why?

## The Answer in One Line
The 51-point intention-action gap is caused by 
trust failure, accessibility barriers, and a 
price perception myth — sustainable accommodation 
is actually 39% CHEAPER than conventional options.

## 6 Root Causes of the Intention-Action Gap
| Barrier | Travelers Affected | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Don't trust sustainability claims | 62% | Trust |
| Hard to find sustainable options | 55% | Accessibility |
| Not a booking priority | 45% | Attitude |
| Think it costs more (wrong) | 40% | Perception |
| Climate change fatigue | 28% | Fatigue |
| Certification confusion | 48% | Information |
